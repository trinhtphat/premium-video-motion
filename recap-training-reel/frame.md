# Design truth — Electric Editorial Recap

## Concept angle

An energetic photo-led memory wall: real training moments move like printed editorial cards while oversized verbs hit on the music grid, ending in a quiet group portrait and a generous thought.

## Canvas and rhythm

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 9:16, 15 seconds.
- Rhythm: **FAST · FAST · HOLD · FAST · FAST · SLOW**.
- Peak: the hands-on `PRACTICE` beat; emotional release: the final group portrait.

## Palette

- Charcoal `#2B2B2B` — structural dark field and ink.
- Off-white `#F9F7F3` — paper field and primary light text.
- Teal `#0E7C7B` — calm connective accent.
- Coral `#F76C5E` — high-energy hit.
- Wasabi `#C6F06B` — electric highlight.

Keep photos/off-white dominant. Use teal/charcoal for structure and reserve coral/wasabi for beat accents.

## Typography

- Display: local Nunito Sans ExtraBold, all caps, tightly tracked, 170–250 px.
- Supporting text: local Be Vietnam Pro SemiBold, 36–54 px.
- Text changes use masked rises, side snaps, or scale slams — never a plain cross-fade.

## Composition

- Focal element: one human moment per beat; crop to preserve faces, hands, and the CHAM setting.
- Edge anchors: small beat index, a short rule/bar, and one colored paper strip.
- Supporting detail: bold color cards, outlined frames, and restrained grain-like paper texture only where it does not obscure faces.
- Background: full-bleed photography with alternating off-white/charcoal editorial panels.

## Motion

- Typography: `kinetic-beat-slam`, with distinct entrances for consecutive verbs.
- Photos: authored 2D pan/zoom pose ladders on inner image wrappers.
- Transitions: hard beat cuts, colored directional wipes, and one photo-card push; no repeated fade sequence.
- End state: `SHARING.` lands large, then `To share is to give.` rises gently and holds for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Audio

- Music: `inspiring-guitar-113bpm.mp3`, cut to roughly 15 seconds from the recommended upbeat section.
- No voice-over.
- Minimal SFX: soft whoosh on two wipes and one restrained hit on the final lockup.
